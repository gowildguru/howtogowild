Elite Status FAQs

Elite Status is an elevated tier of membership within an airline’s loyalty program. Frequent flyers and customers who spend significant amounts on cobranded credit cards can be rewarded with additional perks when they travel. These perks may include things like seat upgrades, added flexibility, and exclusive offers.

You can earn Elite Status by earning a certain number of Status Points during a calendar year. Status Points are different from Travel Miles, which can be redeemed for award flights.

Elite Silver15,000points annually beginning 1/1/2027
Elite Gold25,000points annually beginning 1/1/2027
Elite Platinum50,000points annually
Elite Diamond75,000points annually
Calculate the true cost of Elite Status
Frontier occasionally offers promotions that allow members to receive temporary Elite Status through challenges, credit card sign-up offers, or other promotions. Terms can vary significantly between offers, so it’s important to understand how earning requirements and expiration dates may differ from traditionally earned Elite Status.
Status Points used to qualify for Elite Status are counted beginning January 1 each year and reset the following January 1. Qualifying flights completed by December 31 count toward the previous year’s qualification. When you earn Elite Status, it lasts through the end of the following calendar year. For example, if you earn status in March 2028, it will remain valid through December 2029. If you earn the required number of Status Points again during 2029, your status will then continue through December 2030. Promotional status received through a credit card offer, challenge, or other promotion may have different expiration rules and is not considered traditionally earned status for these purposes.

Status Points

Status Points are an annual count toward your Elite Status achievement. They are earned at different rates depending on what you purchase and may post to your account at different times.

Status Points ARE
  • earned on flight purchases for the member
  • earned on add-on purchases for the member
  • earned on subscription purchases for the member (Discount Den + GoWild! Pass)
  • earned by daily spend on your Frontier Airlines World Mastercard ($1 = 1 Status Point)
  • an annual indicator of your achieved Elite Status; Status Points reset to 0 on January 1 annually
Status Points ARE NOT
  • spent on award flights
  • earned as part of promotions, including credit-card sign-up bonus miles
  • earned on purchases of flights, add-ons, or subscriptions for others

Travel Miles

Unlike Status Points, Travel Miles are redeemed for award flights. While you may earn Travel Miles with the same types of activity, they are used differently.

Travel Miles ARE
  • earned on flight purchases for the member
  • earned on add-on purchases for the member
  • earned on subscription purchases for the member (Discount Den + GoWild! Pass)
  • earned by signing up for the Frontier Airlines World Mastercard
  • earned by daily spend on your Frontier Airlines World Mastercard (1–5x per $1)
  • spent on award flights for the member or friends and family
Travel Miles ARE NOT
  • an indicator of Elite Status
  • reset annually; unlike Status Points, your Travel Miles balance only decreases when miles are spent or expire
  • permanent regardless of activity; Travel Miles expire after one year of no qualifying account activity

*Travel Miles do not reset each January 1. The expiration rule shown here reflects the information carried over from the previous How To GoWild guide.
