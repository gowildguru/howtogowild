Classic interior labels use aircraft delivered before March 23, 2021.

      A320 A320neo A321 A321neo i
      • A320 · Original-generation A320. Frontier’s published layout seats 180.
      • A320neo · Same-length A320 with newer, more efficient engines. Frontier lists 186 seats.
      • A321 · Longer model in the original A320 family. Frontier lists 230 seats.
      • A321neo · The longer A321 with newer, more efficient engines. Frontier lists 240 seats.
      i

      Airbus Airspace is a newer cabin design that can include larger overhead bins, customizable LED mood lighting, and redesigned window surrounds and shades that improve the view.
