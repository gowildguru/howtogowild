A $5 Monday in Denver: Stranahan’s Distillery Tour

Things to Do · Published June 22, 2026

Have $5 and want something neat to do in Denver on a Monday? Put Stranahan’s Distillery on your list. I recently stopped in for their Monday tour and tasting, and it was a fun way to do something a little different without spending much.

When I booked, the code MONDAYS brought the tour price down to just five bucks. That included an in-depth tour of the facility and four whiskey tastings—a pretty solid return on a $5 afternoon.

And yes, you get to keep the taster glass! A tour, a tasting, and a small souvenir for five bucks? I’ll take that.

If Denver is on your next GoWild! itinerary, this is an easy addition to the list. Sometimes the best little travel finds don’t need a big budget.

Check Stranahan’s tour booking page for available dates and current pricing, and try MONDAYS at checkout. The $5 offer and tasting details above reflect my June 2026 visit.
