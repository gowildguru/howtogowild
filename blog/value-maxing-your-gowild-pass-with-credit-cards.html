Value-Maxing Your GoWild! Pass with Credit Cards

Travel Tips · Published May 25, 2026

How do you absolutely VALUE-MAX your next GoWild! trip? For me, the budget-travel trifecta is the Capital One Venture X, the Frontier Airlines World Mastercard, and the GoWild! Pass. Here’s how those benefits can come together from departure to destination.

Start with two checked bags

Before heading to security, check two bags with no additional checked-bag fee using the Frontier card’s primary-cardholder benefit. Book directly through Frontier and follow its cardmember requirements; baggage size and weight limits still apply. Bag prices vary, but I’ve seen booking-time charges around $79 per bag—so this can add up quickly. Frontier bag benefit.

Make security a little easier

Venture X offers up to $120 toward a Global Entry or TSA PreCheck application fee once every four years when you pay with the card. Approval and enrollment are required; expedited screening is not guaranteed every time. It’s an application-fee credit, rather than a $120 charge for every type of membership. Venture X benefit terms.

Trade the boarding area for a lounge

Eligible Venture X primary cardholders can access Capital One Lounges and participating Priority Pass lounges. Activate your Priority Pass membership before traveling. Depending on the location, you may find complimentary refreshments, Wi-Fi, and a comfortable place to work or relax. Amenities and entry availability vary, and guests may cost extra. Priority Pass enrollment and access.

Board with your Elite Gold benefits

If you qualify for and receive the Frontier card’s promotional Elite Gold status, the 2026 benefits include Group 1 boarding and a carry-on for the member. Gold also allows an UpFront Plus upgrade beginning four hours before departure, subject to availability. That upgrade is an opportunity to select an available seat—not a guaranteed automatic upgrade. Check the specific promotion’s qualifying activity and status-validity terms. Frontier Elite benefits.

I’ve seen UpFront seats priced as high as $249 on trips I’ve booked. When an eligible upgrade is available, that can make a low-cost GoWild! booking feel like a very different travel experience.

Put the annual travel credit toward your hotel

Venture X includes a $300 annual credit for bookings through Capital One Travel. If your eligible hotel booking fits within your remaining credit, the credit can cover that booking amount. A more expensive stay leaves a balance to pay, and the credit does not apply to a hotel booked outside Capital One Travel. Capital One Travel credit terms.

Plan for covered delays

Worried about a long delay or an overnight disruption? Venture X’s trip-delay benefit can reimburse up to $500 per purchased ticket for reasonable eligible expenses, such as meals and lodging, when a covered trip is delayed more than six hours or requires an overnight stay because of a covered hazard. A qualifying fare purchase must be charged, at least in part, to the eligible card or its associated rewards program. Coverage is supplemental, and claims require documentation and approval. Venture X benefits guide.

Weather and equipment failure can be covered hazards, but not every disruption qualifies. Missing a connection or finding no GoWild! availability for your preferred return flight does not automatically trigger coverage. Check your card’s benefits guide and confirm how your GoWild! booking qualifies before relying on reimbursement.

Look at the full value

Peace of mind, lounge visits, expedited-security benefits, luggage savings, hotel credits, and possible seat upgrades can make a modest GoWild! ticket go much further. My $15.41 flight example is a per-flight payment—not the total cost of this setup. You still need to account for the pass price, both cards’ annual fees, and any remaining trip costs. Venture X has a $395 annual fee; check the Frontier card’s current pricing and offer terms before applying.

The right combination depends on how often you travel and which benefits you actually use. Pay your statement balance in full: interest charges can quickly erase the value of the perks.

Explore the cards and pass

Sign-up offers and GoWild! promotional pricing change. Compare the terms available when you apply rather than assuming a referral always has the best available offer.
