Frontier's New GoWild! Sale Is Here. But Is It Worth Renewing Through 2028?

GoWild! Pass News · Published November 17, 2026

The 2027 and 2028 GoWild! Pass terms were released today in anticipation of the typical Black Friday renewal deal that has historically appeared around this time. There are a few key takeaways to consider before renewing your pass and a few questions customers may have regarding renewal and the different pass types currently available.

Today's GoWild! Pass Offers

As of today, November 17th, a new passholder can purchase an annual pass for travel from the date of purchase through December 31, 2027, allowing for up to 410 days (13.5 months) of pass usage for $399, with no blackout dates.

Peak-day charges still apply on what were previously blackout dates. The pass is still usable on these dates; however, total fares may exceed $200 depending on demand. So, for $0.97 a day, you get access to unlimited flights when available. This is an attractive proposition for new passholders.

Select current annual passholders may see the option to renew their pass early through December 2028 for $499, extending their pass validity from its current expiration date of March 31, 2027. That's 641 additional days of pass travel, or $0.78 per day. A slightly better per-day incentive than purchasing a new pass outright.

The Fall and Winter Pass is still on sale at $249 for travel from the date of purchase through February 28th, or $2.39 per day of eligible travel. I strongly recommend not purchasing this pass, as it has been on sale for a few months at this point and is not worth it for the short remainder, especially with holiday peak charges in November and December.

The monthly pass is currently available with a $0 first month and a $139 enrollment fee, followed by $149 per month. So, effectively, Frontier is offering a $10 discount on the first month.

While ordinarily a terrible option, in my opinion, for regular travel, for one-off purchases where the same-day or next-day fare is more than $155 (the cost of the first month plus a $16 GoWild! fare), it could present decent savings, assuming you don't intend to use GoWild! any more in the coming months. You can cancel immediately and retain access through the end of the first 30-day period.

The Biggest Concern: GoWild! Availability and Elite Benefits

The thing that worries me most with all of this is that Frontier has really tightened GoWild! inventory. I've observed flights with airline-provided internal load reports showing 20+ seats available but no GoWild! availability in the standard booking window.

As we as consumers refine our practices and leverage the pass for last-minute travel, the airline seems to be playing with when GoWild! is available.

The most important thing to consider is that getting or renewing your GoWild! Pass should not be reliant on a single route or the need to travel for a specific event on a certain date.

Also remember that consumers are being forced into purchasing bundled fares in order to use many elite status benefits in 2027, and the airline controls the cost of a bundle.

There is nothing stopping the airline from charging GoWild! Passholders more for a bundle on a GoWild! fare than on a standard fare. In fact, it's something I've already seen.

Frontier is playing the game, and it's getting harder and harder for consumers to win.

The TL;DR: Is It Worth Renewing?

If you can travel on a basic fare and are fairly loyal to Frontier, the renewal through December 2028 remains attractive.

But it's important to consider that Frontier controls the inventory, and as operating costs change, there's no guarantee that using your elite status benefits will remain affordable or that GoWild! availability will improve.

Since we're being forced into purchasing bundles to access many of our benefits, GoWild! fares are now going to cost more than $16 if you want to use those benefits, and Frontier controls the cost of using what were previously our “free” perks.

Consumers should be especially wary of committing to a pass through 2028 when we've just experienced significant changes to the value of the product we're already paying for.

GoWild! can still be an incredible value, but only if you understand what you're buying and how much control Frontier has over your ability to use it.
