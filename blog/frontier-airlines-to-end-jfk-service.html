Frontier Airlines to End All Services to JFK Airport

Airline News · Published April 28, 2026

Frontier Airlines extended its booking schedule through November 16, 2026, this morning (April 28), but one notable change stands out: the sunset of its final route serving New York JFK.

The last remaining flight, F94731 from JFK to Atlanta (ATL), is now scheduled to depart at 1:00 PM on October 24, 2026. This marks the end of Frontier’s presence at the airport.

Frontier’s stint at JFK has been relatively short-lived. The airline launched inaugural service in early 2024, followed by a rapid expansion in 2025 that included multiple hub routes and transcontinental service to Los Angeles (LAX). However, that growth quickly reversed. By April 2026, Frontier had cut all JFK routes except for a single daily flight to Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (ATL). That final route now appears set to disappear as well.

While Frontier has not explicitly stated the reason for its full exit, the economics of operating at JFK are notoriously challenging. High airport costs, including gate leases, combined with broader industry pressures like fuel prices, make it difficult for ultra-low-cost carriers to sustain service.

Under previous CEO Barry Biffle, Frontier aggressively pursued expansion and a push toward a premium product. More recent strategy shifts under new CEO Jimmy Dempsey have focused on cost control, reducing capacity, and prioritizing profitability over growth.

Nonetheless, Frontier’s exit from JFK ultimately comes as little surprise. The airline is known for quickly entering—and just as quickly exiting—markets that fail to meet its strict financial targets. With the current climate and more conservative approach, I expect we’ll continue to see these cost-trimming efforts to keep Frontier from following in the footsteps of its yellow-liveried competitor.
