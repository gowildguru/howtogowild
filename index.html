    Your Ticket to Success

    Our most frequently asked questions.

    The flight I would like to take is tomorrow but GoWild! is unavailable. Why is this happening?

    GoWild! fare inventory is controlled by the airline. A GoWild! Pass does not entitle you to the last few seats for sale. Expect fuller flights to be unavailable during the standard booking window. Popular dates and routes may not be available if the flight has already filled with customers paying full price.

    A GoWild! Pass is designed primarily to fill otherwise unsold seats.

    Airlines may also sell more tickets than there are seats available. This practice, known as overselling, is common in the airline industry. Airlines estimate how many passengers may no-show or miss a connection and may sell above the aircraft’s seating capacity. When this happens, GoWild! fares may also be unavailable.

    If your travel plans are flexible, keep checking as departure approaches. GoWild! fares can reopen if capacity or booking levels change. Frontier stops selling tickets 60 minutes before departure, and check-in closes at the same time.

    The GoWild! price is showing more than expected during the standard booking window. Why?

    In 2025, Frontier’s system began pricing GoWild! flights according to the departure date of each individual segment.

    For example, a flight departing from Orlando and connecting in Denver may not show the standard GoWild! price until every segment departs “today or tomorrow” in its own local time. That may not occur until 2:00 a.m. ET, when it becomes 12:00 a.m. MT in Denver.

    The same rule applies to itineraries involving a date change. All segments must depart within one calendar day of booking.

    GoWild! is showing available more than one calendar day in advance. Why?

    On a promotional basis, GoWild! availability may open beyond the standard one-day domestic booking window. These promotional periods typically follow demand trends and are more common during off-peak travel dates.

    Promotional GoWild! fares may allow you to purchase flights weeks, or occasionally months, in advance with $0.01 in airfare. Significant early-booking fees may apply when confirming these fares in advance.

    When can I complete my GoWild! booking?

    GoWild! availability for domestic flights opens one calendar day in advance. There is no “24-hour” booking window.

    Flights open at midnight the day before departure in the relevant departure time zone, regardless of whether your flight departs at 12:59 a.m. or 11:59 p.m. For connections, each segment must be within its booking window.

    This is also when any applicable GoWild! Early Booking Fee (GWEF) from an advance-booking promotion will disappear.

    GoWild! fares are showing more than one day in advance at a higher price. Why?

    On a promotional basis, GoWild! fare availability may open beyond the standard one-day domestic booking window. These promotional periods typically follow demand trends and are more common during off-peak travel dates.

    These promotional fares may allow you to purchase flights weeks, or occasionally months, in advance with $0.01 in airfare. Significant early-booking fees may apply when confirming these fares in advance.

    A Peak Day charge may also apply on published blackout dates when GoWild! availability is offered. Unlike an Early Booking Fee, a Peak Day charge does not disappear when the standard booking window opens.

    The flight I want is not available for early booking. Does that mean GoWild! will never be available?

    No. Early booking is capacity controlled like any other fare sale. Once a certain inventory threshold is reached, the promotional GoWild! fare may no longer be offered.

    A flight that is unavailable during an advance-booking promotion may still become available during the normal one-day booking window.

    Keep checking back, as availability can change. In some situations, it may be worthwhile to secure a reasonably priced Standard or Discount Den fare rather than waiting for GoWild! availability.

    Can I travel with friends or family members who also have a GoWild! Pass?

    Yes! Simply search for the number of passengers you’d like to travel with. Before proceeding to the passenger information page, you will be prompted to enter the FrontierMiles number and date of birth for each additional passenger.

    All passengers must have a valid GoWild! Pass on the date of travel. Companion benefits do not apply.

    Can I travel with friends or family members who do not have a GoWild! Pass?

    You can only book a single fare type on a single itinerary. To share elite-status benefits or discounted pricing, such as Discount Den pricing, you must also be traveling under a Standard or Discount Den fare.

    Can I travel with my child who does not have a GoWild! Pass?

    Children cannot be booked on their own using Frontier’s app or website. You must contact customer service to complete a Standard fare booking.

    Reservations: 801-401-9000.

    Can I travel with my pet using my GoWild! Pass?

    Yes. By paying the applicable pet fee, or using eligible Platinum or Diamond benefits, you can travel with an approved pet that fits in a soft-sided carrier under the seat in front of you.

    Passengers traveling with a pet are not permitted to sit in row 1 or in an exit row.
