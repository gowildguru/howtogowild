GoWild! Fare Pricing

GoWild! bookings made during the standard booking window—one calendar day in advance for domestic flights and up to ten calendar days in advance for international destinations—have $0.01 in base airfare plus applicable government and airport taxes and fees per direction.

Every departing segment must be within its applicable booking window to qualify without an Early Booking Fee. Return travel must also qualify when you book it; a return outside the window may need to be booked later.

Wondering “Why isn’t my flight $0.01?” The airfare is only part of the total. The tables below show example totals for one traveler, one direction, during the standard booking window.
